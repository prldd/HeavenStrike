# Dialogue Portrait Prompts for FLUX.2 Klein 4B Distilled

FLUX.2 Klein image editing responds best to one short, concrete instruction. Attach the character's unit art or reference portrait, then paste Section 1, one faction or pool sentence from Section 2, one nature sentence from Section 3, and one emotion sentence from Section 4 as a single paragraph.

Do not paste headings, explanations, unused choices, or negative-prompt lists. Reuse the same reference image and seed when generating every emotion for one character.

## Section 1: Portrait instruction

Create a mid-chest dialogue bust of the attached character. Preserve the exact face, proportions, facing direction, hair, head covering, costume, colors, and identity. Keep a bare source head bare and match framing across every emotion. Render cel-shaded ink-and-gouache RPG art with one-direction lighting on a transparent 1024×1024 canvas.

## Section 2: Faction or pool sentence (choose one)

### Solar

Apply white-gold ceremonial surfaces, blue-black photovoltaic collar inlays, amber illumination, and one clear geometric sun motif.

### Steam

Apply polished brass and copper fittings, green verdigris, one small readable gauge, dark-steel accents, and faint warm light.

### Coal

Apply blackened iron, soot, heat staining, ember cracks, and restrained furnace-orange illumination while keeping the face clear.

### Wind

Apply pale-blue, silver, and white swept-back shapes, small collar vanes or vents, light streamers, and pale-blue illumination.

### Fusion

Apply matte graphite surfaces, restrained neon-green circuit seams, contained conduits, and one small green holographic shoulder accent.

### Universal

Apply the source palette to clean modular forms with steel, charcoal, ivory, muted-blue accents, and soft-white utility illumination.

### Caelis

Apply intact ivory and muted silver-gold imperial forms, sealed ancient technology, and soft white-violet resonance illumination.

### Neutral

Apply practical unmarked cloth or construction in warm gray, soft brown, dull metal, and soft neutral-white light.

## Section 3: Character nature sentence (choose one)

### Automaton

Keep the character mechanical with an enclosed faceplate, lens eyes, and expressive brow panels. Keep a mouthless source mouthless.

### Organic

Keep the character clearly organic with natural skin, hair, eyes, brows, cheeks, nose, and jaw rendered as broad matte painted shapes.

## Section 4: Emotion sentence (choose one)

### Neutral

Show calm attention with level brows, standard eye openness, a relaxed mouth, an upright head, and composed shoulders.

### Happy

Show warm happiness with softly curved eyes, lifted brows, a small natural smile, a slight upward head tilt, and relaxed shoulders.

### Sad

Show quiet sadness with lowered eyes, outward-drooping brows, a small downturned mouth, a lowered head, and withdrawn shoulders.

### Angry

Show controlled anger with sharply narrowed eyes, inward-angled brows, a tense mouth, a slight forward head thrust, and rigid shoulders.

### Surprised

Show sudden surprise with widened natural-sized eyes, raised brows, a small open mouth, a slight backward head movement, and stiff shoulders.

### Determined

Show firm determination with focused eyes, level set brows, a closed mouth, a slight forward lean, and squared shoulders.

### Worried

Show restrained worry with slightly widened eyes, uneven raised brows, a small tense mouth, a slight side tilt, and closed shoulders.

### Smug

Show restrained confidence with half-lidded eyes, one raised brow, a small asymmetric smile, a confident head tilt, and relaxed shoulders.
