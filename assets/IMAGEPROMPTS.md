# Image Generation Prompts

### Visual detail and rendering style

Use the source sprite as the definitive base design.

Retain the essence of the source character, not merely its proportions. The result must feel like the same character converted into a new energy-faction robotic variant, not a different character wearing loosely related styling.

Preserve:
- the source character's recognizable silhouette
- body style, body mass, and proportions
- torso, shoulder, waist, arm, and leg shapes
- clothing or armor silhouette
- head shape and facial proportions
- hairstyle, helmet, and head-accessory silhouette when present
- gender presentation when visually relevant
- perceived strength, rarity, and advancement level
- framing, character scale, and approximate canvas placement
- readability at small in-game size

If gender presentation is clearly readable in the source, preserve it in the robotic redesign. If gender is ambiguous or irrelevant, do not invent or exaggerate gendered traits.

Show the character in a clear three-quarter view:
- not straight-on
- not full side profile
- preserve the attitude and personality of the source stance
- keep the pose compact and readable as tactical RPG unit art
- do not make the pose more aggressive, dominant, or powerful than the source

The body style must match the input. Translate the existing character into robotic materials rather than replacing it with:
- a generic robot chassis
- an exposed endoskeleton
- a bulky war machine
- a thin automaton
- an armored knight unrelated to the source
- a different body type

The character must remain filled out and character-like:
- solid torso and limbs
- continuous outer surfaces
- enclosed joints
- broad readable costume or armor shapes

The face must be clearly robotic but preserve the source head shape, facial proportions, and visual personality.

Use:
- a complete enclosed robotic face
- smooth fitted facial panels
- compact mechanical eyes or optical lenses
- clear brow, cheek, and jaw structure based on the source
- restrained seams and mechanical detail
- readable expression or personality appropriate to the class

Do not create:
- no skull-shaped faces
- no gas-mask faces unless present in the source
- no insect-like heads
- no chibi or childlike faces
- no oversized anime eyes

Match the level of visual detail shown in the style reference.

Use a medium-detail, stylized tactical RPG character-art treatment with:
- large, clean, readable shapes
- simplified armor and costume panels
- broad material areas
- limited panel seams
- only a few clearly visible mechanical details
- restrained highlights and energy effects
- smooth, clean surfaces
- clear anime-inspired game-character proportions
- strong silhouette readability

The unit should look like a distinct game character, not a highly engineered industrial machine or photorealistic robot.

Preserve visual personality through:
- recognizable head shape
- readable mechanical eyes
- clear facial structure
- class-appropriate posture
- source-specific costume shapes
- preserved gender presentation when relevant

Keep most of the body visually simple. Concentrate faction detail in two or three focal areas such as:
- shoulders
- chest
- back
- forearms
- hips
- class equipment

Do not use:
- dense micro-detail
- excessive panel lines
- numerous tiny rivets
- exposed internal machinery
- complex gear assemblies
- dense piping
- layered ornamental filigree
- intricate engraving
- heavy surface weathering
- photorealistic materials
- cinematic concept-art rendering
- hyper-detailed mechanical engineering
- highly reflective metal covering every surface
- excessive glow
- overpowering energy effects

Match the source sprite's existing power level. This is an equivalent faction reinterpretation, not:
- an upgrade
- an evolved form
- an elite unit
- a legendary unit
- a boss
- a stronger combat version

Keep the following proportional to the source:
- body size
- armor mass
- equipment scale
- ornamentation
- mechanical complexity
- emitted light
- energy output
- perceived combat strength

When choosing between additional faction detail and clearer character identity, always prioritize clearer character identity.

Output requirements:
- output the complete unit as a PNG with a true alpha channel
- use a fully transparent background
- no scenery
- no floor
- no platform or pedestal
- no border or frame
- no gradient or colored background
- no large faction emblem behind the unit
- no checkerboard pattern simulating transparency
- no cast-shadow backdrop
- keep the entire character inside the canvas
- preserve comfortable transparent padding around the silhouette
- preserve approximately the same character scale and framing as the source

Create tactical RPG in-game unit art, not splash art.


## Equipment and Weapon Preservation

Treat visible equipment as part of the character's identity, class readability, and silhouette. It is not optional decoration.

Before generating:
- inspect the source sprite carefully for weapons, shields, staffs, books, orbs, relics, focuses, tools, gauntlets, launchers, bows, firearms, and other class equipment
- treat partially obscured, cropped, small, low-detail, or faint equipment as present when its shape, grip, or handling is visible
- preserve the same equipment category
- preserve the same number of items
- preserve the approximate size and visual weight
- preserve the recognizable silhouette
- preserve the grip, placement, orientation, and handling style
- preserve the original gameplay purpose
- create a faction-themed riff on the same equipment rather than copying it literally

Equipment visible in the source must remain clearly visible and readable in the final three-quarter pose.

Do not:
- omit visible equipment
- hide it behind the body
- crop it out
- shrink it into a decorative accessory
- merge it into the armor
- replace it with energy effects
- replace it with a different equipment category
- turn support equipment into an offensive weapon
- turn a small weapon into an oversized elite weapon
- add secondary weapons absent from the source
- create a weapon for a genuinely unarmed source

If the source has paired or multiple weapons, preserve the same number.

If the source is genuinely unarmed and contains no visible class equipment:
- keep both hands empty
- preserve the empty-handed presentation
- do not add, mount, carry, or imply a weapon, shield, staff, focus, tool, or combat device

When class readability depends on equipment, retaining that equipment takes priority over simplifying the design.
